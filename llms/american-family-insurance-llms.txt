# American Family Insurance

> American Family Insurance is a private mutual insurance company headquartered in Madison, Wisconsin,
> offering auto, homeowners, life, renters, umbrella, business, and farm/ranch insurance across the
> United States. It reaches customers through a network of exclusive agents, the MyAmFam mobile app,
> and online self-service.

## API surface

American Family Insurance publishes **no public API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable
contract**. Contract discovery on 2026-09-02 probed every candidate host and path and found nothing:

- No OpenAPI/Swagger at www.amfam.com, b2b.amfam.com or api.amfam.com (`/openapi.json`,
  `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`).
- No GraphQL endpoint.
- No hosted MCP server and no MCP `tools/list` surface.
- No A2A agent card at `/.well-known/agent-card.json` or `/.well-known/agent.json`.
- No `/.well-known/` documents of any kind (see well-known/american-family-insurance-well-known.yml).
- `developer.amfam.com`, `developers.amfam.com`, `dev.amfam.com` and `apis.amfam.com` do not resolve.
- The `amfamlabs` GitHub organization exists but publishes zero public repositories.

`api.amfam.com` resolves and accepts connections — it appears to back the MyAmFam mobile app — but it
is not a documented, published API and nothing about it is offered to third-party developers.

Do not attempt to call an American Family Insurance API. There is no key to obtain and no contract to
read. Integration with American Family is a commercial relationship arranged through the company, not
a self-serve developer surface.

## Company

- [Website](https://www.amfam.com): auto, home, life, renters, umbrella, business and farm/ranch insurance.
- [My Account](https://myaccount.amfam.com/login): policyholder self-service login.
- [B2B Lender Portal](https://b2b.amfam.com): credentialed portal for mortgage lenders and servicers to
  verify coverage, manage escrow billing and coordinate insurance requirements. Access is granted to
  business partners; there is no public API behind it.
- [Contact Us](https://www.amfam.com/contact): customer support channels.
- [Newsroom](https://newsroom.amfam.com/): company news and press releases (RSS at /feed/).
- [Articles](https://www.amfam.com/resources/articles): consumer insurance guidance.
