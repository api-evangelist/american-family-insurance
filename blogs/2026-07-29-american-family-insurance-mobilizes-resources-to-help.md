American Family Insurance mobilized additional Claims team members to assist customers following severe storms that moved through Wisconsin on Monday, July 27, including a tornado that caused significant damage in parts of east-central Wisconsin, including the Appleton area. The National Weather Service declared the tornado an EF-3 after it tore through areas of Appleton, Neenah and Menasha, causing extensive damage, including downed trees and power lines, debris in streets and damage to homes and vehicles.
