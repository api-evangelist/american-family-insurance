National Farm Safety and Health Week, Sept. 21-25, serves as an annual reminder of the importance of protecting farm families, workers and the future of agricultural operations. Throughout the week, farmers are encouraged to prioritize safety and wellness, and long-term planning with life insurance to help strengthen their operations.
