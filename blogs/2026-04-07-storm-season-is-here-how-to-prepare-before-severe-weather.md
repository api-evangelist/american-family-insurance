Spring storms can bring wind, hail and tornadoes. Prepare by monitoring alerts, making a family safety plan, and checking your home (roof, trees, gutters, sump pump and outdoor items) for vulnerabilities. Review your insurance coverages, create a home inventory, and know how to report a claim quickly if damage occurs.
