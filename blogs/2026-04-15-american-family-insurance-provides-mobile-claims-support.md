American Family Insurance is mobilizing additional claims support in Wisconsin after April 13–14 severe storms, including tornadoes, damaging winds, large hail and heavy rain. Field catastrophe adjusters and mobile claims units will help expedite claims and inspections. Two Madison drive-in sites open April 16 by appointment.
